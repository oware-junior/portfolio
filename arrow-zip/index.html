Arrow Zip

A calm, clever arrow puzzle game for Android. Tap snake-like arrows to slide them off the board, but clear the ones in front first. Easy to learn and hard to put down.

Get it on Google Play

Free to play with ads. Made by Junior Kofi Studios.

Screenshots

How to play Arrow Zip

  1. Tap an arrow to slide it out the way it points.
  2. Clear the arrows in front first, because blocked arrows cost a heart.
  3. You have three hearts per level.
  4. Clear every arrow to win.

What's in the game

Endless levels in 5,000+ shapes

Boards grow from tiny to big brain-teasers, in shapes like rockets, cats, castles, owls and guitars, plus letters, numbers, faces and flowers. A new shape appears almost every level, and every fifth level is a Hard level with many more arrows.

Daily challenge

A new challenge every day, with streaks to keep.

Stars and achievements

Earn up to three stars per level, beat your best time, build combos with quick clears and unlock 40 achievements.

Also included

Hints that light up an arrow that can move, a short interactive tutorial, light and dark themes, and pinch to zoom on big boards. It works on phones and tablets.

Questions

Is Arrow Zip free?

Yes. Arrow Zip is supported by ads, and an optional Remove Ads subscription turns them off.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

Trying again is always free. If you'd rather keep your progress, you can choose to watch an ad for one more heart.

Where can I download Arrow Zip?

Arrow Zip is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.
