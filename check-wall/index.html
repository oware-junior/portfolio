Check Wall

A neon strategy board game for Android. Race your pawn to the far side of the board, or drop a wall to block your rival. Easy to learn, hard to master.

Get it on Google Play

Free to play with ads. Made by Junior Kofi Studios.

Screenshots

How to play Check Wall

  1. On your turn, move your pawn one square or place a wall.
  2. Walls slow your opponent down, but can never trap them completely.
  3. Jump over your rival when you meet face to face.
  4. The first player to reach the other side wins.

What's in the game

Endless campaign

Beat computer opponents one by one, each smarter than the last, with a boss battle every 10 levels. Earn up to 3 stars per level.

Play the world

Ranked online 1v1 matches with a turn clock, country flags, friends, rematches, emoji reactions and a global leaderboard.

Learn from every move

Replay each match move by move. Every move is graded from brilliant to blunder, and you can see the better move you missed.

Also included

Pass & Play on one phone, a 2-minute interactive tutorial, and dark and light themes. Play as a guest, or sign in with Google to save your progress.

Questions

Is Check Wall free?

Yes. Check Wall is free to play with ads. You can remove ads with an optional monthly or yearly subscription.

Where can I download Check Wall?

Check Wall is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Add players you meet as friends and challenge them online, or play Pass & Play on one phone.
