About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Ghana Communication Technology University (GCTU). Most of my work is mobile development with Flutter: taking a UI idea and turning it into an app that runs smoothly on both Android and iOS.

My coursework covers hardware, networking and low-level programming, and that background shapes how I write software. I care about what's happening underneath, whether that's a widget tree or a loop written in assembly.

Right now I'm learning more about automation workflows, AI-assisted development and backend design, so I can build complete products end to end.

Android game · Junior Kofi Studios

Check Wall

A neon strategy game I designed and shipped: race your pawn across a 9×9 board and block your rival with walls. An endless campaign against computer opponents that get smarter every level, real-time online matches with a turn clock, reconnect protection and emoji reactions, global leaderboards with win/loss records, Google sign-in and a Remove Ads subscription. Runs on Android 7.0 and newer, and I ship updates from tester feedback.

Arrow Zip

A relaxing puzzle game I designed and built on my own. Tap an arrow and it slides out along its path. Levels never end: each one is generated and guaranteed solvable, across more than 5,000 board shapes, from rockets and cats to letters, faces and flowers. Every fifth level is a hard one, plus best times, daily challenges and 40 achievements.
